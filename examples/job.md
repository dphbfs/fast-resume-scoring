Senior Backend Engineer (Go)

Lumen Freight is building the routing and pricing platform behind same-day
freight for small businesses. Our services quote, book, and track tens of
thousands of shipments a day across North America.

## What you'll do

- Design and build Go services for quoting, booking, and carrier dispatch.
- Own services in production: on-call rotation, incident reviews, and SLOs.
- Model event flows on Kafka and keep them reliable under peak load.
- Work with product and data teams to ship pricing experiments safely.
- Mentor engineers through code review and design discussions.

## Requirements

- 5+ years of backend software engineering experience.
- Strong experience with Go in production.
- Experience designing REST or gRPC APIs.
- Solid PostgreSQL skills, including schema design and query tuning.
- Experience running services on Kubernetes in a cloud environment (AWS or GCP).
- Familiarity with event streaming (Kafka or similar).
- Experience with observability tooling such as Prometheus, Grafana, or OpenTelemetry.

## Nice to have

- Experience with logistics, marketplaces, or pricing systems.
- Terraform or other infrastructure as code.
- Python for data tooling.

## Benefits

- Remote within the US or Canada, with quarterly team meetups.
- Health, dental, and vision coverage.
- Learning budget of $1,500 per year.
